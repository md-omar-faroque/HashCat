Who We Are & What We Do

Hashcat is a highly versatile and powerful password recovery and hash cracking tool that is widely used in the field of cybersecurity. Developed by Hashcat Cofounders Adnan Habib, Kevin Quintero, Youssef Kabbouch, Sahadat Sayem. Hashcat is known for its speed and ability to crack a variety of password hashes through various attack methods. It supports a wide range of hashing algorithms, making it a valuable tool for security professionals, penetration testers, and system administrators.

It's important to note that while Hashcat can be a valuable tool for legitimate security purposes such as password auditing and penetration testing, its usage should comply with ethical standards and legal regulations. Unauthorized or malicious use of Hashcat or similar tools is strictly prohibited. Always ensure proper authorization and adherence to ethical guidelines when using such tools.
